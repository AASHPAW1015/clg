# 🎡 Wheel of Impossible Destiny

### *Spin it. Regret it. Spin it again.*

A neon physics arcade where a glowing wheel decides your fate, and the wheel has questionable judgment. Fling it, watch the sparks fly, and find out whether you win free pizza (imaginary) or absolutely nothing.

Built as a single `index.html` file with plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript. No libraries, no downloads, no sound files. Every beep and boom is generated live in your browser.

---

## 🕹️ How to Play

1. Open the live link above (works on desktop and phone).
2. **Drag the wheel** and let go to fling it, or hit the **SPIN** button, or press **Space**.
3. Listen to the pegs click as the pointer bounces over them.
4. Wait for the wheel to stop. Your destiny gets announced with explosions.
5. Spin while it's already moving to give it an extra shove.

**Buttons**

- **SPIN** starts a random-power spin.
- **Chaos** makes the wheel get random speed kicks mid-spin. Good luck predicting anything.
- **Sound** turns the synth effects on or off.

## 🤫 Secret Features

- **The Mind Changer:** sometimes the wheel is going full speed, then suddenly spins the other way. It does this on purpose.
- **Poke the "?":** click the middle of the wheel **7 times** quickly and the wheel files a complaint about you.
- **Konami Code:** press ↑ ↑ ↓ ↓ ← → ← → B A to unlock **Impossible Mode** (chaos on, wheel at max speed, gravity flips).
- **Result Effects:** each outcome has its own effect, including glitch, color inversion, rainbow mode and anti-gravity confetti.

---

## 🧪 Under the Hood

- Real spin physics with friction, pegs that slow the wheel, and a spring-loaded pointer.
- Canvas particle system with shockwaves and screen shake.
- Web Audio API for all sound effects.
- `segmentAt()` figures out which slice is under the pointer, and it has `console.assert()` unit tests at the bottom of `index.html` (open the Console to check that they pass).
